# Personal IP Hub — Product Charter v1

## 1. Product Definition

Personal IP Hub is Lyra Wang's long-term public personal value profile and digital identity hub.

It is not merely:
- an online resume;
- a GitHub portfolio;
- a personal blog;
- a list of skills, certificates, or tools;
- a collection of social links.

Its purpose is to help both people and machines understand:
- who Lyra is;
- what kind of person she is;
- what she has actually built;
- how she works;
- how she learns;
- how she has evolved;
- what value she can create for others.

## 2. Primary Product Goals

### Goal A — Discoverability

Help search engines, AI systems, and agents find and accurately understand Lyra Wang as a consistent public entity.

Key questions:
- Can a machine determine who Lyra Wang is?
- Can it identify her official website and public profiles?
- Can it understand what projects, writing, research, and evidence belong to her?
- Can it distinguish first-party facts from unsupported claims?

### Goal B — Persuasion

Help human visitors move from first contact to meaningful interest.

Target journey:

Find → Understand → Trust → Prefer → Act

The site should help a visitor:
1. quickly understand who Lyra is;
2. understand what value she can create;
3. see credible evidence;
4. understand how she thinks and works;
5. become interested in continuing to follow, contact, collaborate with, or evaluate her.

## 3. Primary Audiences

The first version should work for visitors who do not already know Lyra, including:
- interviewers and hiring managers;
- potential collaborators;
- AI / product / digital-transformation peers;
- GitHub visitors;
- readers from public writing channels;
- people interested in her projects, research, or working methods;
- AI agents and search systems retrieving public facts about her.

## 4. Core Product Principle

Do not optimize for "looking impressive."

Optimize for:

Authenticity → Clarity → Evidence → Relevance → Trust → Beauty

The site should show:
- real work;
- real decisions;
- real evolution;
- real learning;
- real evidence.

Avoid unsupported positioning, invented metrics, inflated seniority, fabricated outcomes, or decorative technical complexity.

## 5. Value Communication Model

Every important page or content asset should answer two layers.

### Machine Layer
- Who?
- What?
- When?
- Role?
- Source?
- Evidence?
- Relationship to other entities?

### Human Layer
- Why does this matter?
- What problem was being solved?
- What decision was made?
- What changed?
- What was learned?
- Why should the reader care?

## 6. Core Identity Model

The site should present Lyra through five connected layers:

### Identity
Who I am and what kind of person I am.

### Value
What problems I can help structure, solve, or move forward.

### Proof
What I have actually built, written, researched, or shipped.

### Method
How I frame problems, work with AI, make decisions, review work, and iterate.

### Evolution
How my capabilities and judgment developed over time.

Projects, GitHub, WeChat, reading, research, writing, and public activity are evidence channels supporting these five layers.

## 7. Content Philosophy

Prefer:
- "What I built"
- "Why I built it"
- "What changed"
- "What I decided"
- "What I learned"
- "How this may help others"

Over:
- generic skill lists;
- tool-logo walls;
- self-ratings;
- vague personal branding language;
- claims without evidence.

## 8. Scope Principle

The product is Static-first, not Static-only.

Default to static pages and content.

Only introduce dynamic services when a real requirement appears, such as:
- user login;
- data write-back;
- comments;
- live personalization;
- AI conversation;
- real-time data.

Do not add a database, CMS, backend server, or infrastructure merely for technical sophistication.

## 9. Deployment Principle

Target public domain:

lyrawang.bamamei.online

Preferred architecture:

GitHub → Static Build → Vercel / Cloudflare Pages → CDN → Custom Domain

The hosting provider is not yet frozen, but the site should remain portable and not depend on a traditional self-managed server.

## 10. Success Test

A first-time visitor should be able to answer within a short session:
- Who is Lyra?
- What value does she create?
- What has she actually done?
- How does she work?
- How does she learn?
- How has she grown?
- What evidence supports these claims?
- What should I do next if I want to know more?
